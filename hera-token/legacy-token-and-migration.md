# Legacy Token & Migration

Hera Token completed a 1:1 contract migration on **October 22, 2026**.

The migration replaced the previous Ethereum and Metis HERA contracts with the new generation of the Hera Token.

All eligible holder balances, staked balances and pending staking rewards included in the migration snapshot were accounted for and distributed at a 1:1 ratio to the corresponding EVM addresses.

No manual claim was required.

Legacy HERA contracts are retained here for historical and verification purposes only.

They should not be considered active HERA assets and users should not purchase, bridge or otherwise interact with them as a way of acquiring the current Hera Token.

Prices, liquidity values or portfolio valuations associated with legacy contracts may become inaccurate or fall to zero as external services complete their migration updates. These values do not represent the market data of the new HERA token.

### Migration Information

The migration was executed using a snapshot-based accounting process rather than requiring users to manually migrate their balances.
